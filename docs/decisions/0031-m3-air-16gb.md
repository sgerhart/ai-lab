# ADR 0031 — M3 Air unified memory is 16 GB

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Supersedes:** the unconfirmed-RAM clause of ADR 0011 and open decision D-015

## Context

ADR 0011 recorded 16 GB from `sysctl` (2026-09-19) as unattested. The owner attested 16 GB on 2026-09-21.

## Decision

The M3 Air has **16 GB** unified memory. It remains the human plane and must not host always-on control-plane compose.

## Consequences

- Confirmed hardware tables may list 16 GB for `m3-air`.
- Do not size M1 Postgres/Qdrant against the Air’s 16 GB.
